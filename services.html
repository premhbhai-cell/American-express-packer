Service Questions

How do I get a moving quote?

Call or WhatsApp us on 7877289006 or 8905524937. Share your current and new location, the items and your preferred date, and we will give you an estimate.

Do you handle packing materials too?

Yes. We use cartons, bubble wrap, stretch film and protective covers so your goods stay safe during transit.

Can you shift furniture and heavy appliances?

Yes. Our team is trained to dismantle, wrap, load and re-assemble furniture and to move heavy and fragile items carefully.

Do you move cars and bikes?

Yes, we offer vehicle transport along with household shifting. Contact us for details for your route.

Which areas do you serve?

Our Head Office is in Zirakpur (Mohali), and we serve Zirakpur, Mohali, Chandigarh, Panchkula, along with intercity moves across India.

Is storage available?

Yes, we can arrange short and long-term warehousing when your new place is not ready yet.
